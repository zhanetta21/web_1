About Us

The story of a small idea that grew into a cafe.

How we started

The Bloom began with a simple observation: morning coffee rarely brings real joy. We decided to change that by pairing quality bean roasting with the aesthetic of a flower workshop. Here is how our journey unfolded:

  1. The idea and first drink tastings at home
  2. Developing the menu and finding a local bean supplier
  3. Renovating and designing the first room in a botanical style
  4. Opening The Bloom on Abay Street
  5. Launching table and VIP room reservations for guests
