About The Bloom

A small idea that grew into a cafe

The Bloom began with a simple observation: morning coffee rarely brings real joy.

We decided to change that by pairing quality bean roasting with the aesthetic of a flower workshop. Our cafe combines specialty coffee, handmade desserts and a warm botanical atmosphere.

Our Story

  1. The idea and first drink tastings at home
  2. Developing the menu and finding a local bean supplier
  3. Renovating and designing the first room in a botanical style
  4. Opening The Bloom
  5. Launching table and VIP room reservations
